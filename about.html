About EQQN

Earthquake Quick News Research Center

Mission

The mission of the Earthquake Quick News Research Center (EQQN) is to advance the understanding of seismic activity through rigorous scientific research, timely analysis, and transparent communication. We aim to foster a collaborative environment where scientists and researchers can share vital data and findings.

Vision

We envision a world where high-quality earthquake research is easily accessible, where rapid analysis of seismic events leads to better preparedness, and where scientific communities work seamlessly across borders to mitigate earthquake risks.

Research Areas

  • Seismology & Geophysics
  • Earthquake Engineering & Structural Analysis
  • Seismic Hazard Assessment
  • Early Warning Systems & Monitoring
  • Disaster Risk Reduction
  • Machine Learning Applications in Seismology

What EQQN Does

EQQN serves as an independent scientific repository and research publication platform. We:

  • Publish peer-reviewed and working research papers.
  • Provide a directory of verified researchers and their academic contributions.
  • Analyze and report on recent earthquake events from a scientific perspective.
  • Host datasets and computational models for seismic research.
  • Archive historical earthquake predictions and methodologies for retrospective study.

Scientific Integrity & Research Publication Process

EQQN upholds strict scientific integrity. All research submissions undergo a structured review process by our administrative and scientific editorial team before publication. Researchers submit their work as drafts, which are subsequently reviewed for methodological soundness, data accuracy, and contribution to the field. EQQN clearly distinguishes between preliminary research, verified findings, and historical predictions.
